Modernizing Security Management Across India

Apna Guard is a next-generation security management platform built to transform traditional security operations into a smart, digital, and transparent system. We help societies, commercial buildings, and enterprises replace manual registers with real-time digital security workflows.

Our platform connects guards, admins, and residents through powerful mobile and web applications. From visitor management to guard patrolling and attendance tracking, Apna Guard ensures complete visibility, faster decision-making, and improved safety across all premises.

Simple, Smart & Secure Workflow

Apna Guard connects guards, admins, and residents on a single platform, ensuring smooth
coordination and complete transparency across all security operations.

1

Guard App Entry

Guards use the Apna Guard mobile app to record visitor entries, scan QR codes for patrolling, mark attendance, and report incidents instantly.

2

Approval & Verification

Residents or administrators receive instant notifications to approve or deny visitor access. All approvals are time-stamped and logged securely.

3

Activity Tracking

Every action—visitor entry, patrol round, attendance, and parcel delivery—is automatically tracked and stored for real-time monitoring and reporting.

4

Dashboard & Reports

Admins access a centralized dashboard to view live activities, download reports, monitor guard performance, and ensure compliance.
